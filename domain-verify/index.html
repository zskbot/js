What happens next?
  1. Generate a unique TXT value above.
  2. Sign in to the registrar or DNS provider that controls your domain.
  3. Add the TXT record exactly as shown. Do not change nameservers or existing records.
  4. Wait for DNS propagation, then return here and check again.

DNS lookup uses Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint. Your generated challenge is held only in this browser tab; it is not sent to OCode.js servers.
